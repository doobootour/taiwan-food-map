Where Steak Doesn't Break the Bank

Steak in Taiwan

From cheap cast-iron steaks to upscale steakhouses

View This Category's Food Map ↓ See Other Categories

Taiwan has a whole culture built around cheap sizzling-plate steak, with sets that include soup, a salad bar, and even ice cream for a very reasonable price. Upscale steakhouses are well established too, for when you want to spend more.

What Makes It Special?

01

Budget sizzling steak

Cheap chain steak sets often include an unlimited salad bar, soup, and drinks — great value.

02

Doneness

The default leans well-done, so be explicit if you want medium-rare.

03

Watch the hot plate

Steaks arrive sizzling on a hot plate — sauce can splash, so dress accordingly.

How to Enjoy It

  • ✓Budget chains are best ordered as a full set (soup + salad bar + main)
  • ✓Be specific and clear about your preferred doneness
  • ✓Upscale steakhouses need weekend reservations
  • ✓Watch young kids around the hot sizzling plate
