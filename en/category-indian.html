A Spice-Forward Escape

Indian in Taiwan

Curry, tandoor, and reliable vegetarian options

View This Category's Food Map ↓ See Other Categories

Indian and Nepali immigrant-run restaurants are established in Taiwan's major cities, making curry, tandoori chicken, and naan easy to find. Vegetarian menus are usually well developed, a plus for plant-based travelers.

What Makes It Special?

01

Solid vegetarian options

Most Indian restaurants have a clearly marked vegetarian section — useful for veg travelers.

02

Spice levels

Many places var you pick a spice level on a scale.

03

Lunch buffets

Some spots run a weekday lunch buffet, a good way to sample several curries at once.

How to Enjoy It

  • ✓Vegetarians should look for '素食/Veg' labels on the menu
  • ✓Be clear about your spice level when ordering
  • ✓Check whether naan or rice is included, or an extra
  • ✓Confirm lunch buffet days/hours ahead of time
