Pasta in Taiwan

From casual pasta joints to authentic Italian dining

View This Category's Food Map ↓ See Other Categories

Taiwan's pasta scene splits into two: cheap, casual spots aimed at students and office workers, and fine dining that goes for authentic Italian. Casual sets usually bundle in soup and a drink for good value.

What Makes It Special?

01

Lunch sets

Weekday lunch sets — pasta, soup, and a drink — are often a bargain.

02

Spicy pasta

Taiwan-style mala or chili pasta is common — worth a try if you like heat.

03

Reservations

Authentic Italian fine dining often requires a weekend reservation.

How to Enjoy It

  • ✓Check for lunch-set hours (roughly 11:30am–2pm)
  • ✓Casual spots often seat you without a wait
  • ✓Book fine dining at least 1–2 days ahead
  • ✓Portions can run smaller than expected — consider a side dish
