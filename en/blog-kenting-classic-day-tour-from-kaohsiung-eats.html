Kenting Classic Day Tour: What to Eat at Taiwan's Southern Tip

Before a tour from Kaohsiung to Eluanbi Lighthouse and Kenting's beaches, check what to see and nearby restaurants.

Published 2026.11.23

Taiwan restaurant picks gathered and curated from real reviews by Korean travelers, by a local who has lived in Taiwan for 20 years.

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialKenting · Sights & Food

Kenting (墾丁) is at the southernmost tip of Taiwan's main island, where coastline formed by uplifted coral reef meets the sea. It's far from Kaohsiung, so a tour is easier for seeing it in a day.

01 · What Are Kenting and Eluanbi Lighthouse?

Kenting is protected as a national park for its landscape of forest growing on coral reef uplifted from the sea. Eluanbi Lighthouse (鵝鑾鼻燈塔) is a white iron-tower lighthouse built in 1881. It's the southernmost on Taiwan's main island and Taiwan's only armed lighthouse, with fort-like walls and a moat.

02 · What Not to Miss

✓

Eluanbi Lighthouse After an upgrade in 1962 it was called the 'Light of East Asia (東亞之光)'. It has been open to the public since 1992, and you can stroll the park around it and look out to sea.

✓

墾丁大街 This area's night market street, where stalls and shops line up at night.

✓

墾丁 beach A beach known as '大灣'.

04 · Nearby Restaurants in Kenting

There's one shaved ice shop right near Eluanbi Lighthouse, and the rest are 7–12 km away. Distances below are straight-line from the coordinates on our map, so the actual route may be longer.

1

悟空冰舖 · Shaved Ice · about 0.3 km northwest A shaved ice shop in Hengchun. The mango shaved ice is tasty.

2

Alex Reggae Bar · BBQ · about 7.6 km northwest A charcoal barbecue bar. The som tam is good too.

3

亞發師海景餐廳 · Seafood · about 12.2 km northwest A seafood restaurant on the 2nd floor of a seaside fish market in Kenting. A large sashimi platter is NT$200; English menu available and cards accepted.

Each place has its own hours and closing days, so check the hours on Google Maps before you go.
