Hot Pot Heaven

Hot Pot in Taiwan

From solo hot pot to mala-style spice bombs

View This Category's Food Map ↓ See Other Categories

Taiwan has perfected the solo hot pot — individual pots make it easy to eat alone without feeling awkward. Broths range from fiery mala to mild bone or tomato bases, and many spots throw in an unlimited drink and topping bar.

What Makes It Special?

01

Solo hot pot

Many shops serve single-portion mini pots, making it easy for solo travelers.

02

Split broth

Order a yuanyang (split) pot to get spicy and mild broth side by side.

03

Unlimited bars

Some places offer unlimited drinks, ice cream, and sauces — great value.

How to Enjoy It

  • ✓Traveling solo? Confirm individual-pot service first
  • ✓Decide your spice level (mild/medium/hot) when ordering
  • ✓All-you-can-eat meat is often time-limited
  • ✓Most places add a 10% service charge
