Yehliu & Shifen Coastal Trail

Taipei Suburbs (Yehliu, Shifen, Shifen Waterfall, Jinguashi, Jiufen) Travel Guide

From dramatic rock coastlines to lantern-lit alleys

View This Region's Food Map ↓ See Other Regions

A hillside town just an hour by bus from Taipei, famous for its lantern-lit stepped alleys and misty mountainside views — it's said that a huge share of every Taiwan trip's photos come from here. It pairs perfectly with nearby Yehliu, Shifen, and Jinguashi for a full day trip.

Traveler Favorites

01

Yehliu Geopark (野柳)

A coastal geopark famous for the Queen's Head rock — wave-carved formations that look almost otherworldly.

02

Shifen Sky Lanterns

Write a wish on a sky lantern and release it right over the old railway tracks — often the highlight of a Jiufen day trip.

03

Shifen Waterfall (十分瀑布)

Often called "Taiwan's Niagara” — close to the Shifen lantern village, easy to combine in one visit.

04

Gold Ecological Park, Jinguashi (金瓜石)

A preserved Japanese-era gold-mining town — you can even touch a giant gold bar on display.

05

Jiufen Old Street

A lane of teahouses and street food along Jishan Street — most beautiful just as the lanterns switch on at dusk.

Things to Do Here

  • ✓Explore the strange rock formations at Yehliu
  • ✓Ride the Pingxi rail line through old mining villages
  • ✓Release a sky lantern and make a wish at Shifen
  • ✓Visit the Gold Ecological Park in Jinguashi
  • ✓Watch the sunset glow over Jiufen's old street
