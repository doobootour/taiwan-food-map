Shoushan Zoo: Getting There, Tickets, Kids Tips & Nearby Eats

Admission NT$40, closed Mondays, and keep snacks in your bag. Getting there, kids tips, Klook tickets, and nearby eats in one guide.

Published 2026.10.24 · Updated 2026.10.31

Taiwan restaurant picks gathered and curated from real reviews by Korean travelers, by a local who has lived in Taiwan for 20 years.

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialKaohsiung · Sights · With Kids · Food

Looking for a half-day outing with kids in Kaohsiung? Shoushan Zoo (壽山動物園) comes up often. It sits on the slope of Shoushan in Gushan District, is reachable by bus from downtown, and tickets are inexpensive. This guide is based on the zoo's official visitor, transport and facilities pages as of October 2026, and also lists nearby restaurants from our map for after your visit.

01 · Shoushan Zoo hours and tickets at a glance

ItemDetails (as of October 2026)
HoursTue–Sun 09:00–17:00, last entry 16:30
ClosedMondays and Lunar New Year's Eve (除夕); open if a Monday is a national holiday
StandardNT$40
Age 6 to under 18NT$20 (ID checked)
FreeUnder 6, 65 and over, and others (ID checked)
Address高雄市鼓山區萬壽路350號

No booking is needed: buy at the ticket booth or a ticket machine on the day. To buy ahead, the official site lists ibon, KKday and Klook. Payment by iPASS (一卡通), EasyCard (悠遊卡), credit card, LINE Pay, iCash (愛金卡) or cash. Reduced and free tickets require ID, so bring passports for kids and seniors and ask at the booth whether they apply.

02 · Getting to Shoushan Zoo: bus 56 is easiest

1

From Kaohsiung Station Catch the zoo bus 56 on Zhongshan 1st Rd (中山一路, near Lane 325) in front of Kaohsiung railway station.

2

MRT Yanchengpu (鹽埕埔) Leave Orange Line Yanchengpu by Exit 4, turn right onto Wufu 4th Rd (五福四路) for about 10 m, and board bus 56 at the stop in front of Hua Nan Bank (華南銀行).

3

Light rail Wenwu Temple (文武聖殿) Walk along Dagong Rd (大公路) to Gushan 1st Rd (鼓山一路), turn right and go about 50 m to the Taipower (台電) stop. On the way back, get off at Jianguo 4th Rd (建國四路口) and walk 250 m back to Shoushan Park (壽山公園) light rail station.

✓

Every 40 min weekdays, 20 min weekends Bus 56 runs daily 08:30–17:30, with an extra Yanchengpu-to-zoo service every 10–15 minutes on weekends and holidays. It does not run on Mondays, when the zoo is closed.

✓

Bus 219 means an uphill walk From bus 219 you get off at Gushan District Office (鼓山行政中心) and walk up Wanshou Rd (萬壽路), so with kids or older parents bus 56 is the better choice.

FacilityHow it works (as of October 2026)

Children's farm feedingTue–Sun 10:00–11:00 and 14:00–15:00, cancelled in rain, ends when the hay runs out
Water play plaza (親水廣場)Outdoor splash area, closed Thursday mornings for cleaning
Service centreBy the entrance: stroller, wheelchair and anti-monkey backpack rental, 3 nursing rooms

There are six toilet blocks, including by the service centre, the water play plaza and the lion enclosure. The water play plaza can change with weather and maintenance, so check the latest news on the official site before packing spare clothes.

04 · Watch out for the macaques: keep food in your bag

✓

Keep food out of sight Shoushan has many wild macaques, and you'll meet them up close inside the zoo too. Keep food in your bag, not in your hand or hanging from the stroller.

✓

Don't eat while walking The official advice is to eat indoors. The restaurant area is a macaque hotspot, so take extra care there.

✓

If one grabs, let go If a macaque snatches food, let go rather than pulling back. Avoiding long eye contact is also an official rule.

✓

Not allowed in No pets (not even in a bag), and no bicycles, toddler tricycles, scooters or inline skates. The whole zoo is non-smoking.

Bus 56 also stops near Wenwu Temple (文武聖殿) light rail station, so see how to ride the Kaohsiung Light Rail for tickets. You can pair the zoo with the Kaohsiung Museum of Fine Arts by light rail, and if Taipei is on your trip too, read our Taipei Zoo shuttle and tickets guide. 05 · Nearby restaurants after your visit

Distances below are straight-line from the coordinates on our map, so the actual route may be longer.

1

Song Dang Yeshi Restaurant 送噹夜市餐廳 · Thai · about 0.9 km south Map note: The best Thai milk tea I've had so far, and our child loved the crispy-outside, juicy-inside grilled chicken.

2

Laodian Baihong Lurou Rice, Yancheng Branch · Taiwanese · about 1.0 km southeast Map note: A Michelin Bib Gourmand pick full of locals; 'happiness for about 5,000 won'.

3

Aiji Breakfast · Breakfast · about 1.1 km southeast Map note: A steady stream of locals getting takeout; recommends the crispy danbing.

4

柒壹喫堂x鹽海小餐館 · Taiwanese · about 1.2 km southeast Map note: Staff kindly explained the menu. The broth, somewhere between seolleongtang and tom yum, plus the corn and squid dishes, were unique and tasty.

5

京華餐廳 · Hot Pot · about 1.3 km southeast Map note: Went on local friends' strong recommendation. Every dish was tasty, and the pickled cabbage (酸菜) hot pot is said to be especially famous.

6

Yancheng Wu Family Kumquat Douhua · Shaved Ice · about 1.4 km southeast Map note: The kumquat douhua goes really well with hot weather.

7

Liu's Traditional Rice Ball & Danbing Shop · Breakfast · about 1.4 km southeast Map note: A breakfast spot with lots of locals calling in and taking out; the turnip cake and rice balls are tasty.

Each place has its own hours and closing days, so check the hours on Google Maps before you go.
