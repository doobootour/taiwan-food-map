Kavalan Distillery in Yilan: Tickets, Tours and a Cake Café Half-Day

The once-free visit now has a ticket — but you can spend the full amount on tastings or souvenirs.

Published 2026.10.10 · Updated 2026.10.31

Taiwan restaurant picks gathered and curated from real reviews by Korean travelers, by a local who has lived in Taiwan for 20 years.

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialYilan · Day Trip

"Do I have to pay to visit Kavalan now?" Yes. Kavalan Distillery (噶瑪蘭威士忌酒廠) has charged admission since March 1, 2026. It's at No. 326, Sec. 2, Yuanshan Rd., Yuanshan Township, Yilan County, reachable by bus or taxi from central Yilan. Here's a half-day plan based on Kavalan's official ticket, tour and transport pages and the café's own notices.

01 · Kavalan Distillery Tickets at a Glance

PriceWho
GeneralNT$200General visitors
ConcessionNT$100Junior/senior high students, Yilan County residents, 65+ (ID required)
FreeNT$0Children 12 and under, visitors with disabilities (plus one companion)

The full ticket price can be credited toward tastings and purchases on site (as of October 2026). Check the current opening hours on Google Maps before you go.

02 · Free Guided Tours: English Daily at 11:00

✓

Ticket first Free guided tours start after you buy a ticket. English runs daily at 11:00, Chinese at 10:00 and 16:00, Japanese at 13:00 and Korean at 14:00 (as of October 2026).

✓

Register online Tours meet at a set time and place, so register with the form at the bottom of the official reservation page. With more than 80 people, the tour may switch to an audio broadcast.

✓

About 60 minutes A company video, the distillery production line, then the paid tasting area and gift shop on the first floor. You can't tour the factory after drinking, so save tastings for afterward.

03 · Getting There from Yilan Station

From → ToNotes
Bus 752 (宜蘭勁好行)Yilan Transfer Station (宜蘭轉運站) → 員山農會成功分部5-min walk; return from the 金車酒廠 stop

If you plan to taste, skip the rental car and take the bus or a taxi.

04 · On the Way Back: A Yilan Cake Café

Back in Yilan city, stop at the cake café 花轟小姐 (Cake.Coffee.Dessert). The Yilan shop is at No. 285, Taishan Rd., and a Jiaoxi branch (No. 225, Sec. 1, Zhongshan Rd., Jiaoxi) opened in October 2026.

1

Walk-in only No reservations, and everyone 6 and up orders at least one drink.

2

90 minutes when busy If people are waiting, seating is limited to 90 minutes.

3

Don't go too late Desserts change daily and the shop closes early when they sell out. Days off are posted on its Facebook and Instagram, so check the current opening hours on Google Maps before you go.

"Tour first, taste later, dessert last." — a half-day summary based on the official guidance.
