Lihpao Land (麗寶樂園) Tickets and Nearby Eats: Where to Eat After the Park

Enjoy the theme park, water park and Ferris wheel, then pick a restaurant in Houli or Fengyuan in one go.

Published 2026.11.25

Taiwan restaurant picks gathered and curated from real reviews by Korean travelers, by a local who has lived in Taiwan for 20 years.

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialTaichung · Sights & Food

Lihpao Land (麗寶樂園) is a large resort-style theme park in Houli (后里), northern Taichung. It's away from the city, so deciding where to eat afterwards helps.

01 · What Is Lihpao Land?

It presents itself as 'two parks, water and land', combining the land theme park 探索世界 (Discovery World) and the water park 馬拉灣 (Mala Bay), with the 天空之夢 Ferris wheel as well. The address is No. 8, 福容路, 后里區, Taichung.

02 · What Not to Miss

✓

搶救地心 The park's signature thrill ride, known as 'the world's only broken-track roller coaster'.

✓

熊大的麗寶小鎮 A family zone that's good for playing with kids.

✓

天空之夢 Ferris wheel The Ferris wheel at Lihpao Resort (麗寶樂園渡假區).

✓

Mala Bay is seasonal The Mala Bay water park opens seasonally. Check operating days on the official site before you go.

03 · Getting There

1

From Houli (后里) Station Walk from Houli Station to the front of 后里國小 to catch the park's free shuttle bus. Buses 155 and 811 also go there.

2

By car About 3 minutes from the 后里/外埔 interchange on 國道1號.

The closest place is about 1.2 km from the park, and the rest are 5–8 km away in Houli and Fengyuan (豐原), so a car or taxi is easier. Distances below are straight-line from the coordinates on our map, so the actual route may be longer.

2
