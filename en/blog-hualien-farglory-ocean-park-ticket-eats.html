Farglory Ocean Park Tickets: What to Eat in Hualien City Afterwards

Before visiting the hilltop ocean theme park with sea views, check the hours, how to get there and Hualien city restaurants.

Published 2026.11.17

Taiwan restaurant picks gathered and curated from real reviews by Korean travelers, by a local who has lived in Taiwan for 20 years.

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialHualien · Sights & Food

Farglory Ocean Park (遠雄海洋公園) is an ocean theme park on a seaside hill in Shoufeng Township (壽豐鄉), Hualien. There are no restaurants on our map nearby, so we've laid out a route back to Hualien city to eat afterwards. Based on the official 遠雄海洋公園 site, as of October 2026.

01 · What Is Farglory Ocean Park?

An ocean theme park with eight themed zones and four shows. The address is No. 189, 福德, 鹽寮村, 壽豐鄉, 花蓮縣. Inside the park there are also three themed restaurants and a 19th-century English harbour-style shopping street.

02 · What Not to Miss

✓

Dolphin show The park's signature show.

✓

Sea lion show The Sea Lion Patrol (海獅巡邏隊) show.

✓

海洋探索館 An exhibition hall for seeing marine life up close.

✓

奇妙海洋慶典 A show held at the Crystal Palace.

03 · Getting There

1

Bus From Hualien Station, take bus 1140 or 1145 and get off at the 海洋公園 stop. About 35 minutes, about NT$51.

2

Taxi About 25 minutes from Hualien Station, about NT$400.

3

By car Take 台9線 across 花蓮大橋, then follow 台11線.
