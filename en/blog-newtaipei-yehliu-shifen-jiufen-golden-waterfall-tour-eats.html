Yehliu, Shifen, Shifen Waterfall & Jiufen Day Tour: What to Eat in Jiufen

Before a tour of four northern sights in a day, check what to see at each stop and the restaurants in Jiufen's alleys.

Published 2026.11.03

Taiwan restaurant picks gathered and curated from real reviews by Korean travelers, by a local who has lived in Taiwan for 20 years.

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialNew Taipei · Sights & Food

This day tour combining the northern coast and mountain villages near Taipei visits Yehliu (野柳), Shifen (十分), Shifen Waterfall (十分瀑布) and Jiufen (九份) in turn. They're hard to fit into one day by public transport, so a tour is easier, and eating dinner in Jiufen's alleys at the last stop keeps the route simple. Based on 交通部觀光署 information, as of October 2026.

01 · What Are the Four Tour Stops?

Yehliu is a coastline of mushroom rocks and tofu rocks carved by seawater and wind, and Shifen is a village with shops lining both sides of the 平溪線 railway. Shifen Waterfall is a curtain-shaped waterfall near Shifen village, and Jiufen is a hillside mining town that boomed after gold was found around 1890.

02 · What Not to Miss at Each Stop

✓

Yehliu's '女王頭' A mushroom rock whose profile resembles a queen. Along the path to the tip of the cape you can see many other rock shapes.

✓

Shifen sky lanterns Releasing a sky lantern (天燈) with your wishes written on it from the railway tracks is the signature experience.

✓

Shifen Waterfall (十分瀑布) A curtain-shaped waterfall called 'Taiwan's Niagara'. It's close to Shifen village, so it's an easy next stop after releasing a sky lantern.

✓

Jiufen's 基山街 and 豎崎路 Food stalls line the narrow 基山街 alley, and teahouses hung with red lanterns line the stone steps of 豎崎路.

04 · Restaurants on Jiufen Old Street

Places on our map cluster inside Jiufen Old Street, the last stop of the tour. All are 0.1–0.2 km apart in the alleys, so you can walk to them during free time. Distances below are straight-line from the coordinates on our map, so the actual route may be longer.

1

安迪藥燉排骨 · Taiwanese · about 0.1 km north Map note: A Jiufen noodle shop with refreshing noodle soup and juicy dumplings at low prices.

2

石頭貓 · Café · about 0.1 km northwest Map note: A cat goods shop and café in Jiufen. The honey pomelo tea is tasty and drinks are reasonably priced.

3

阿柑姨芋圓 · Shaved Ice · about 0.1 km southwest Map note: A famous Jiufen taro ball (芋圓) shop. The taro ball shaved ice is great when you get thirsty walking around.

4

魚丸伯仔 · Noodles · about 0.1 km northeast Map note: A fish ball soup shop in Jiufen. Fish ball soup with dry mixed noodles makes a good order. One item per person is required.

5

微光森林 Twilight Cafe · Café · about 0.2 km southeast Map note: A café in an alley above Jiufen Old Street, with good coffee and breakfast in a forest-like setting.

Each place has its own hours and closing days, so check the hours on Google Maps before you go.
