Xiaoliuqiu Turtle Snorkeling: Which Beach and What Rules?

Donggang ferries, Zhong'ao's nesting-season night beach ban, DBNSA turtle-viewing rules, and picking a licensed operator — what to check before you enter the water.

Published 2026.10.16 · Updated 2026.10.31

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialNear Kaohsiung · Xiaoliuqiu Guide

"Which beach should I pick to see turtles on Xiaoliuqiu?" Xiaoliuqiu (小琉球) is a coral island southwest of Pingtung; the ferry from Donggang (東港) takes about 25–30 minutes. Before picking a beach, learn the nesting-season night ban and the turtle-viewing code. This guide follows Liuchiu Township and Dapeng Bay National Scenic Area (DBNSA) pages, as of October 2026.

DBNSA asks visitors to choose licensed operators and follow the turtle-viewing code. For DIY snorkel zones, check Pingtung County and DBNSA notices before you go.

02 · To Donggang, Then the Ferry

✓

Zuoying HSR → 9127D From THSR Zuoying, take Taiwan Tourist Shuttle 9127D and alight at the Tungliu Pier stop. Official fare info lists NT$120 one-way (checked October 2026).

✓

Ferry ~25–30 minutes Liuchiu Township says the passenger boat from Donggang takes about 25–30 minutes. Private Tungliu boats arrive at Baisha Visitor Pier; the public ferry arrives at Dafu Fishing Harbor.

✓

Train + highway bus From Kaohsiung or Pingtung stations, take a highway bus to Donggang, then walk about 10 minutes to the pier.

✓

Recheck sailings the same day Schedules change with weather and operations. Recheck Tungliu and public-ferry notices before you leave.

03 · Zhong'ao Beach and the Nesting-Season Night Ban

1

Where Zhong'ao is DBNSA describes Zhong'ao as the wide white beach left of Baishawei visitor pier, behind Baisha Elementary — also listed for swimming and beach activities.

2

Night ban May 1–Oct 31 Green turtles nest here. Each year from May 1 to October 31, from 20:00 to 05:00 the next morning, entry to the protected beach and intertidal zone is banned.

3

Keep nights quiet and dim During nesting season, avoid night beach activity when possible; keep noise down and avoid strong lights.

✓

Beauty Cave & Vase Rock Both appear on DBNSA's sights list. Choose your snorkeling spot by following a licensed operator's route.

04 · Turtle Rules and Snorkeling

✓

Turtle-viewing code Do not touch, catch, startle, chase, or encircle turtles. Do not block their path; stay at least 5 m away; do not feed them.

✓

Licensed operators only For snorkeling and diving, DBNSA recommends operators with a valid business registration. A licensed list is on the DBNSA site.

✓

Life jacket and buddy Government diving guidance (including snorkeling) recommends a CNS-compliant life jacket plus snorkel, mask, and fins, and a buddy system.

✓

Protected species & reporting Sea turtles are Class I protected wildlife. Harassment can mean up to one year in prison or a fine of NT$60,000–300,000. Report violations to the Coast Guard at 118.

05 · Getting Around the Island

✓

Taiwan Tourist Shuttle 601 The loop bus starts at the visitor pier and stops near Beauty Cave and Vase Rock. EasyCard, iPASS, and iCash work; a day pass is sold on the bus.

✓

E-bikes have separate rules Scooters and e-bikes have plate, insurance, and license rules. Read the Xiaoliuqiu e-bike rental guide before you rent.

✓

A transfer in Kaohsiung On transfer days, the Kaohsiung–Tainan YouBike tips post can help with short hops.
