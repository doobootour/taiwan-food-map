Sicao Green Tunnel Bamboo Raft: Where to Eat After the Ride

Float through the mangrove tunnel by raft, then pick a restaurant in nearby Anping in one go.

Published 2026.11.03

Taiwan restaurant picks gathered and curated from real reviews by Korean travelers, by a local who has lived in Taiwan for 20 years.

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialTainan · Sights & Food

The Sicao Green Tunnel (四草綠色隧道) is a raft ride through mangrove waterways in Annan District (安南區), Tainan. There are no restaurants on our map nearby, so heading to Anping (安平) to eat afterwards is easiest. Based on information from 雲嘉南濱海國家風景區 and 臺南旅遊網, as of October 2026.

01 · What Is the Sicao Green Tunnel?

Behind 四草大眾廟, mangroves over 50 years old grow on both sides of a drainage channel and cover it like a tunnel. It's described as Taiwan's only green waterway of its kind. One loop takes about 30 minutes.

02 · What to See from the Raft

✓

Four mangrove species Four species of mangrove grow here: 五梨跤, 水筆仔, 欖李 and 海茄苳.

✓

Mudflat creatures You can see fiddler crabs (招潮蟹) and mudskippers (彈塗魚) under the mangroves.

✓

四草大眾廟 The starting point is a temple over 300 years old. Next to it is a small exhibition hall with a sperm whale specimen.

03 · Getting There

1

Starting point Board at the pier next to 四草大眾廟, No. 360, 大眾路, 安南區, 臺南市.

2

What you get Before boarding you're given a life jacket and a conical hat for shade.

05 · Anping Restaurants for After the Ride

There are no restaurants on our map near the green tunnel. The closest are about 2.9–3.9 km away in Anping, so a taxi or car is easier. Distances below are straight-line from the coordinates on our map, so the actual route may be longer.

1

煉‧瓦 日式餐廳 · Japanese · about 2.9 km southeast Listed as Japanese on the map.

2

同記安平豆花 安平總店 · Bakery · about 3.0 km southeast Listed as bakery on the map.

3

Anping Bean Jelly (同記安平豆花 安平2店) · Taiwanese · about 3.4 km southeast Map note: An old tofu dessert shop near Anping's 德陽艦, consistently tasty enough to have many regulars.

4

Chou’s Shrimp Rolls (周氏蝦捲 老店) · Seafood · about 3.5 km southeast Map note: Said the fried shrimp is 'tasty but greasy since it's fried', and urged 'you have to try it'.

5

阿財牛肉湯 (A-Cai Beef Soup) · Beef Noodles · about 3.6 km southeast Map note: Called the beef with water spinach 'really delicious', and said the beef soup meat is more tender than at Hang Yuan Beef Noodles in Kaohsiung.

6

La Belle Maison Cafe法國傳統手工甜品咖啡餐廳 · Café · about 3.7 km southeast Listed as café on the map.

7

Meller 墨樂咖啡 · Café · about 3.9 km southeast Listed as café on the map.
