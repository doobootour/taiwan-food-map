A Crossroads of Southeast Asian Flavor

Thai in Taiwan

Sour, sweet, and spicy — Taiwan's Thai food scene

View This Category's Food Map ↓ See Other Categories

Thanks to a sizable Southeast Asian migrant community, Thai food is well established in Taiwan. Options range from crowd-pleasers like tom yum and pad thai to community favorites with more authentic flavor.

What Makes It Special?

01

Adjust the spice

Some places tone it down for local tastes — ask for it 'Thai spicy' if you want the real heat.

02

Lunch sets

Weekday lunch sets (fried rice, pad thai) are often good value.

03

Community favorites

Restaurants in migrant-worker hubs tend to serve more authentic flavors.

How to Enjoy It

  • ✓Settle on your spice level when ordering
  • ✓Take advantage of lunch-set hours (roughly 11:30am–2pm)
  • ✓You can ask for cilantro (香菜) to be left out
  • ✓Community-area restaurants tend to be cash-only
