National Palace Museum Tickets and Nearby Eats: Where to Eat After Your Visit

Tour the imperial treasures, then pick the museum's own restaurant or a spot in Shilin in one go.

Published 2026.11.11

Taiwan restaurant picks gathered and curated from real reviews by Korean travelers, by a local who has lived in Taiwan for 20 years.

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialTaipei · Sights & Food

Ask for a good place to spend half a rainy day in Taipei and the National Palace Museum (國立故宮博物院) comes up first. It's in 外雙溪, away from the city centre, so it helps to decide in advance whether to eat inside the museum or head out to Shilin. Based on the official 故宮 site and 交通部觀光署 information, as of October 2026.

01 · What Is the National Palace Museum?

A museum of imperial collections handed down from the former imperial palace (故宮). It was built in 1965 in 外雙溪 in Chinese palace style, and holds about 700,000 objects inherited from the collections of four dynasties: Song, Yuan, Ming and Qing.

02 · Pieces Not to Miss

✓

翠玉白菜 A jade carving shaped like a cabbage, the most popular piece in the collection.

✓

肉形石 A stone that looks like a piece of pork.

✓

毛公鼎 A bronze vessel inscribed with a long inscription.

✓

至善園·至德園 The Song-style garden and pond garden next to the museum are also nice to walk around.

03 · Getting There

1

Bus from MRT 士林 Station Take a bus from MRT 士林 Station and get off at the 故宮博物院 stop.

2

Visiting tips The collection is huge, so allow half a day. For a more detailed route, see our National Palace Museum visiting tips.

05 · Nearby Restaurants for After Your Visit

Sanxitang, the museum's 4th-floor restaurant, is on our map, so it's a good place for a break mid-visit. The rest are 2.4–2.8 km away in Shilin and Dazhi, so a bus or taxi is easier. Distances below are straight-line from the coordinates on our map, so the actual route may be longer.

1

三希堂 Sanxitang (National Palace Museum 4F restaurant) · Café · about 0.1 km north Map note: Of the museum's 4th-floor restaurant: 'I'm someone who cares about atmosphere, so I liked it here quite a lot.'

2

Chiou Hwa · Dim Sum · about 2.4 km southeast Listed as dim sum on the map.

3

三明堂 · Bakery · about 2.5 km northwest Listed as bakery on the map.

4

Bao Bao Bao · Dim Sum · about 2.5 km west Map note: Near Shilin Station. Generous portions; the shrimp fried rice and creamy shrimp were especially good. Would go back.

5

Butler Coffee House · Café · about 2.6 km south Map note: Tucked in a corner of the market, but the Brazil-blend iced Americano is so creamy it deserves high praise.

6

D23 Coffee Dazhi · Café · about 2.6 km south Listed as café on the map.

7

Niu Mowang Steak House 牛魔王牛排館 · Steak · about 2.8 km southwest Map note: A steak place at Shilin Night Market. The recommended tenderloin steak is tasty.

Each place has its own hours and closing days, so check the hours on Google Maps before you go.
