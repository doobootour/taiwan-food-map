A Shopping Break Between Bites

Shopping in Taiwan

From night-market shopping to local designer boutiques

View This Category's Food Map ↓ See Other Categories

These are shopping stops that fit neatly around a food-focused itinerary — cheap night-market finds to local designer boutiques around Ximending and Da'an.

What Makes It Special?

01

Night-market shopping

Clothes and accessories are cheap at night markets, though not every stall has fixed prices.

02

Discover local brands

Ximending and Da'an have a cluster of Taiwanese designer boutiques worth browsing.

03

Tax-free shopping

Spend over a certain amount and you can use the tourist tax refund (TRS) scheme.

How to Enjoy It

  • ✓Look for a TRS (tax refund) sticker at eligible shops
  • ✓Night-market prices vary by stall — worth comparing a few
  • ✓Payment method (cash/card) varies by shop — check ahead
  • ✓Keep receipts in case you need them for a tax refund
