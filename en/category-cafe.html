Taichung, the Café-Hopping Capital

Café in Taiwan

From specialty coffee bars to design-forward cafés

View This Category's Food Map ↓ See Other Categories

Taiwan — Taichung especially — is famous for its café density. Options range from specialty-bean roasteries to converted heritage buildings, and most places var you linger without pressure to rush.

What Makes It Special?

01

Taichung café-hopping

Cafés cluster around Taichung's Ximen Road and Yizhong Street.

02

One drink per seat

Many cafés require at least one drink order per person seated.

03

Outlets and wifi

Laptop-friendly cafés usually post outlet and wifi availability at the entrance.

How to Enjoy It

  • ✓Popular cafés get busiest in the afternoon (2–5pm)
  • ✓Check the entrance for seating time limits (often 2 hours)
  • ✓Confirm the one-drink-per-person policy
  • ✓Be mindful of other guests' space when taking photos
