A City That Comes Alive After Dark

Bar in Taiwan

From craft cocktail dens to laid-back local beer pubs

View This Category's Food Map ↓ See Other Categories

Taipei's cocktail bar scene regularly lands on Asia's best-bar lists. You'll find everything from unmarked speakeasy-style bars to casual craft beer pubs.

What Makes It Special?

01

Speakeasy bars

Many bars hide behind unmarked entrances — part of the fun is finding them.

02

Check for a cover charge

Some popular bars have a minimum order or cover charge.

03

Taiwanese craft beer

Beyond Taiwan Beer, more pubs now carry local craft brewery beers.

How to Enjoy It

  • ✓Reservations are recommended for popular bars on weekends
  • ✓Confirm any minimum order or cover charge ahead of time
  • ✓Bring ID — age verification may be requested
  • ✓Check last-train times before planning your night
