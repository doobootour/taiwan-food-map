The Taiwanese Morning Ritual

Breakfast in Taiwan

Soy milk, rice rolls, and egg pancakes — how locals start the day

View This Category's Food Map ↓ See Other Categories

Breakfast is practically its own industry in Taiwan. Shops selling soy milk, egg crepes (dan bing), and rice balls (fan tuan) open before dawn, and most business is takeout, so lines move fast.

What Makes It Special?

01

Hot or cold soy milk

Choose warm, savory soy milk or the cold, sweet version.

02

Customize your dan bing

Many shops var you add cheese, tuna, or corn inside the egg crepe.

03

It's mostly takeout

Locals mostly grab and go — seating is often limited.

How to Enjoy It

  • ✓Hours are usually short — dawn to around 11am
  • ✓Menus are often Chinese-only; a photo lookup beforehand helps
  • ✓Specify takeout vs. dine-in when ordering
  • ✓Many old-school shops are cash-only
