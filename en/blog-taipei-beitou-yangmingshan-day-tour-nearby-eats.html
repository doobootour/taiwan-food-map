Beitou Hot Springs & Yangmingshan Day Tour: What to Eat in Xinbeitou

Before doing volcanic Yangmingshan and the hot spring town of Beitou in one day, check what to see and restaurants near Xinbeitou.

Published 2026.11.21

Taiwan restaurant picks gathered and curated from real reviews by Korean travelers, by a local who has lived in Taiwan for 20 years.

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialTaipei · Sights & Food

Behind Beitou (北投), the hot spring town in northern Taipei, rises the volcanic landscape of Yangmingshan (陽明山). On a tour that combines the two, eating in the hot spring town fits the route naturally.

01 · What Are Yangmingshan and Beitou?

Yangmingshan National Park centres on the 大屯 volcano group, so volcanic cones, craters, fumaroles and hot springs are its main scenery. Xinbeitou (新北投) on its slopes is a hot spring town of hot spring hotels and public baths, and Thermal Valley (地熱谷) beside Beitou Park (北投公園) is one of the sources of Beitou's hot springs.

02 · What Not to Miss

✓

Thermal Valley (地熱谷) Sulphur steam rises all year, so it's also called '地獄谷'. The spring water reaches 98℃ and has a pale green colour.

✓

小油坑 A post-volcanic activity zone about 805 m above sea level, where you can see fumaroles and sulphur crystals, with volcanic peaks such as 七星山 and 大屯山 in view.

✓

擎天崗 Open grassland where you can see water buffalo and grassland ecology.

05 · Nearby Restaurants in Beitou

Places on our map cluster 0.7–1.1 km from Thermal Valley, toward Xinbeitou Station. Distances below are straight-line from the coordinates on our map, so the actual route may be longer.

1

雙月食品社 北投店 · Taiwanese · about 0.7 km west Map note: A popular Beitou restaurant. The wait can be long, but turnover is fast, so it's worth waiting.

2

Ootoya Xinbeitou Restaurant · Japanese · about 0.8 km west Listed as Japanese on the map.

3

Orange Tea (健康橘子工坊) · Café · about 0.9 km west Map note: A drink shop on the way to the Beitou foot bath. Drinks with various kinds of pearls are tasty.
