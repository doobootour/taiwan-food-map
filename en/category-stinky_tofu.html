Taiwan's Most Divisive Signature Dish

Stinky Tofu in Taiwan

Fermented tofu with a smell you'll never forget — worth trying at least once

View This Category's Food Map ↓ See Other Categories

Stinky tofu is famously the most polarizing dish in Taiwan. Despite the pungent fermented smell, once fried and served with pickled cabbage or sauce, it's surprisingly savory and crispy — most travelers who try it end up won over.

What Makes It Special?

01

Fried vs. mala style

Choose between the common night-market fried version and the mala-broth-boiled style.

02

Pair with pickled cabbage

Taiwanese-style pickled cabbage is the classic pairing and cuts through the richness.

03

Smell isn't taste

The flavor is milder and more savory than the smell suggests — worth trying with an open mind.

How to Enjoy It

  • ✓If you're smell-sensitive, choose an indoor shop over an open-air stall
  • ✓First time? Start with the fried version
  • ✓Eat it with whatever pickles or sauce the shop provides
  • ✓Smell can linger on clothes or in transit — double-bag it for takeout
