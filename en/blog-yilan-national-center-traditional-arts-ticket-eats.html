National Center for Traditional Arts Tickets and Nearby Eats: Where to Eat After Your Visit

Explore the old street and workshops, then pick a restaurant in nearby Wujie or Luodong in one go.

Published 2026.11.15

Taiwan restaurant picks gathered and curated from real reviews by Korean travelers, by a local who has lived in Taiwan for 20 years.

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialYilan · Sights & Food

The National Center for Traditional Arts (國立傳統藝術中心) on the Dongshan River (冬山河) in Yilan (宜蘭) is a complex recreating an old street where you can enjoy crafts and performances. It sits apart on the riverside, so deciding on a nearby restaurant in advance makes the day easier. Based on 交通部觀光署 information, as of October 2026.

01 · What Is the National Center for Traditional Arts?

A national cultural space created to preserve and promote Taiwan's traditional arts. It faces 親水公園 across the 冬山河, and workshops, exhibition halls and performance venues are gathered in a complex recreating an old street.

02 · What Not to Miss

✓

傳藝老街 Watch traditional craft demonstrations and try them yourself.

✓

黃舉人宅·文昌祠 An old mansion and a temple are inside the complex.

✓

曲藝館 A venue for traditional performances. It's more fun if you go on a day with scheduled shows or activities.

03 · Getting There

1

Bus from Luodong (羅東) Station Take Taiwan Tourist Shuttle (台灣好行) 綠21 or 葛瑪蘭 bus 621.

04 · Nearby Restaurants for After Your Visit

There are no restaurants on our map right outside the Center. The closest are 2.4–3.4 km away, and the next are over 4.5 km away in central Luodong, so a taxi or bus is easier. Distances below are straight-line from the coordinates on our map, so the actual route may be longer.
