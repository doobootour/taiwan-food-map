Harbor City Energy

Kaohsiung Travel Guide

Fiery southern flavors where the Liuhe Night Market meets harbor charm

View This Region's Food Map ↓ See Other Regions

A port city in southern Taiwan with a slower, more relaxed pace than Taipei — wide boulevards and a light rail line give it an unusually open, easygoing feel. With plenty of waterfront sunset spots, it's often called the “mood city” of southern Taiwan.

Traveler Favorites

01

Lotus Pond (蓮池潭)

A lake park famous for its Dragon & Tiger Pagodas — one of southern Taiwan's most photogenic spots.

02

Pier-2 Art Center (駁二藝術特區)

Old harbor warehouses turned art district, full of galleries, concept shops, and cafés.

03

Cijin (旗津) Beach

A 10-minute ferry ride away — great for cycling past the seafood street.

04

Liuhe Night Market (六合)

Kaohsiung's signature night market, with more locals than tourists for an authentic feel.

05

85 Sky Tower & Love River (愛河)

A riverside walk that's perfect at sunset, with especially beautiful night views.

Things to Do Here

  • ✓Ride the light rail around the harbor city
  • ✓Cycle along Cijin Beach
  • ✓Take photos around the Pier-2 Art Center
  • ✓Watch the sunset from a café on the Love River
  • ✓Try local late-night snacks at Liuhe Night Market
