A Bakery Around Every Corner

Bakery in Taiwan

From original pineapple cake bakeries to design-forward bread shops

View This Category's Food Map ↓ See Other Categories

Taiwan's bakery scene runs from traditional pineapple cake to Japanese-style soboro bread to European-style salt buns. Famous brands wrap gifts beautifully, making bakeries a popular souvenir stop too.

What Makes It Special?

01

Sample before you buy

Well-known pineapple cake shops often var you try a sample before purchasing.

02

Popular items sell out early

Certain items at Taipei's best bakeries can sell out by late morning.

03

Gift packaging

Many bakeries offer special gift-box packaging, great for souvenirs.

How to Enjoy It

  • ✓Check the shelf life on gift pineapple cake and buy accordingly
  • ✓Morning visits give the best chance at popular items still being in stock
  • ✓Be considerate when sampling before buying
  • ✓Confirm how long the packaging keeps items fresh at room temperature
