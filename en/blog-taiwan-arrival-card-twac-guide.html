Taiwan Arrival Card (TWAC): When and How Should You Fill It Out?

The latest rule — the window grew from 3 days to 7 days before arrival — plus how families can register everyone at once.

Published 2026.10.02

Taiwan restaurant picks gathered and curated from real reviews by Korean travelers, by a local who has lived in Taiwan for 20 years.

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialTaiwan Travel · Entry Guide

"Will they hand out arrival cards on the plane?" Since October 1, 2025, Taiwan no longer accepts paper arrival cards; you declare online through the Taiwan Arrival Card (TWAC). Based on official National Immigration Agency notices and real traveler experiences, here's when and how to fill it out.

01 · Then vs. Now

Before (paper card)Now (TWAC, as of Sep 2026)
HowOn paper, on the plane or at the airportOnline on the official site
WhenRight before immigrationWithin 7 days before arrival (since July 15, 2026; previously 3 days)
Family/groupsOne card eachOne person can submit for up to 16 people
At immigrationHand in the cardNothing to show — scanning your passport pulls up the data
CostFreeFree

02 · How to Fill It Out

1

Go to the official site Fill it out at twac.immigration.gov.tw in Chinese or English; field guidance is available in seven languages including Korean.

2

Upload a passport photo A photo of your passport data page auto-fills your name and passport number.

3

Enter flight and hotel Have your arrival flight number and Taiwan hotel address ready.

4

Keep the confirmation email You don't need to show it at immigration, but a screenshot is reassuring.

03 · Common Mistakes

✓

Submitting too early You can't submit more than 7 days before arrival. Do it the week you fly.

✓

Paid third-party sites Search results include sites that charge fees, but the official TWAC is free. Make sure the address is immigration.gov.tw.

✓

Missing a companion After submitting for your group, confirm everyone is on the list. You can still edit online afterward.

✓

If you didn't do it beforehand Arrival airports post TWAC QR codes so you can fill it out on the spot, but it takes time when lines are long.

04 · Who Doesn't Need It

TWAC is for foreigners entering visa-free or on a visitor visa. Holders of a Taiwan residence permit don't need to file it. If you're visiting as a tourist on a foreign passport, assume you need it.

"Took five minutes the night before my flight." — a summary of reviews from travelers who filled out TWAC in advance.
