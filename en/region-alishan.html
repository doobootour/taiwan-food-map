Sunrise Above the Clouds

Alishan / Sun Moon Lake Travel Guide

A mountain escape linking Alishan and Sun Moon Lake

View This Region's Food Map ↓ See Other Regions

Taiwan's signature mountain getaway, famous for sunrise above the clouds and its narrow-gauge forest railway. Pair the ancient cypress forests of Alishan National Scenic Area with nearby Sun Moon Lake for a great overnight trip.

Traveler Favorites

01

Alishan Sunrise (祝山日出)

Sunrise over a sea of clouds from Zhushan viewing platform, reached by an early-morning sunrise train.

02

Alishan Forest Railway

A mountain railway running since 1912 — the stretch through cypress forest is the scenic highlight.

03

Sacred Trees Area (神木群)

A walking trail past cypress trees thousands of years old — perfect for forest bathing.

04

Sun Moon Lake (日月潭)

Taiwan's largest lake — popular for a boat cruise or a full bike loop around the water.

05

Alishan High Mountain Tea (高山茶)

A high-mountain oolong tea region grown in the misty peaks — tour a tea farm and sample fresh brews.

Things to Do Here

  • ✓Ride the sunrise train and watch dawn break at Zhushan
  • ✓Ride the forest railway through the cypress woods
  • ✓Cycle the full loop around Sun Moon Lake
  • ✓Sample high-mountain tea at a local farm
  • ✓Take a forest-bathing walk through the ancient cypress grove
