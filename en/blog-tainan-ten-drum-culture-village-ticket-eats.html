Ten Drum (十鼓) Culture Village Tickets and Nearby Eats: Where to Eat After the Drum Show

Watch a drum show in a former sugar factory, then pick a nearby restaurant from our map in one go.

Published 2026.11.13

Taiwan restaurant picks gathered and curated from real reviews by Korean travelers, by a local who has lived in Taiwan for 20 years.

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialTainan · Sights & Food

Ten Drum (十鼓) Culture Village is a former sugar factory in Rende District (仁德區), Tainan, turned into a drum performance venue. It's away from the old town, so deciding where to eat afterwards makes the day easier. Based on the official 十鼓文創園區 site, as of October 2026.

01 · What Is Ten Drum Culture Village?

車路墘製糖所, opened in 1909, became 仁德糖廠 and closed in 2003; 十鼓擊樂團 then turned the site into a cultural space centred on drum performances. Twenty-two Japanese-era warehouses remain on the roughly 7.5-hectare site.

02 · What Not to Miss

✓

Drum show One ticket covers one drum show. Watching another costs an extra NT$300.

✓

Old sugar factory warehouses Twenty-two Japanese-era warehouses remain, so you can explore the factory atmosphere as it was.

✓

魔法工廠 One of the sights inside the village.

✓

Extreme activities Independent travellers are told they can use the extreme activity facilities without limit once inside.

03 · Getting There

1

TRA Bao'an (保安) Station You can walk there by turning from 文賢路 onto 文華路 and crossing the tracks. You can also take bus 紅3 (toward 臺南都會公園) from the 保安 transfer station and get off at the 十鼓文創園區 stop.
