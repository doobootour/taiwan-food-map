Gaomei Wetlands, Miyahara & Rainbow Village Tour: What to Eat in Central Taichung

Before a tour of a sunset spot, a mural village and an old eye clinic building, check out restaurants near Miyahara.

Published 2026.10.30

Taiwan restaurant picks gathered and curated from real reviews by Korean travelers, by a local who has lived in Taiwan for 20 years.

My Secret Taiwan Eats EditorialTaichung · Sights & Food

Among Taichung's (臺中) sights, Gaomei Wetlands (高美濕地), Miyahara (宮原眼科) and Rainbow Village (彩虹眷村) are far apart, so a tour is the easy way to combine them in one day. There are many restaurants on our map around Miyahara, next to Taichung Station, so it's a good place for a meal. Based on 交通部觀光署 and 臺中觀光旅遊網 information, as of October 2026.

01 · What Are the Three Stops?

Gaomei Wetlands is an ecological reserve of about 701.3 hectares south of the 大甲溪 estuary, visited by more than 120 bird species. Miyahara is a former eye clinic built in 1927 that the 日出 team turned into a dessert shop, and Rainbow Village is a small village where Grandpa 黃永阜's paintings cover the alley walls and ground.

02 · What Not to Miss

✓

Sunset at Gaomei Wetlands The sun setting behind the wind turbines by the sea is the most famous view. The boardwalk over the mudflats opens according to the tides.

✓

Miyahara's bookshelf displays The bookshelf-style displays reaching the ceiling are the highlight, and ice cream and gift sweets like pineapple cake are popular.

✓

Rainbow Village The colors are vivid wherever you point the camera, so it's great for photos. You can see it in 30 minutes to an hour.
