The Joy of Grilling

BBQ in Taiwan

Charcoal grills and all-you-can-eat Korean-style BBQ

View This Category's Food Map ↓ See Other Categories

BBQ in Taiwan splits into Taiwanese/Japanese-style charcoal grilling and Korean-style all-you-can-eat spots. The buffet-style places (吃到飽) are especially popular for group outings and usually run on timed seatings.

What Makes It Special?

01

Timed all-you-can-eat

Buffet BBQ seatings usually run 90–120 minutes.

02

Solo-friendly grills

Many spots have individual grill seating, so solo diners aren't left out.

03

Use the side bar

Unlimited drink and dessert bars are common — make the most of them.

How to Enjoy It

  • ✓Buffet spots require one order per person and reservations are recommended
  • ✓Going over the time limit may incur extra charges
  • ✓Check ahead for solo-friendly individual-grill seating
  • ✓Leftover food usually can't be boxed up — some places even charge a food-waste fee
