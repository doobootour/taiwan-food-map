A Slice, Taiwan-Style

Pizza in Taiwan

From wood-fired pizzerias to Taiwan-topped pies

View This Category's Food Map ↓ See Other Categories

Taiwan's pizza scene splits between authentic Neapolitan wood-fired specialists and casual chains with distinctly local toppings like sweet-potato mousse or potato. Delivery culture is strong, so it's easy to eat one back at your room.

What Makes It Special?

01

Wood-fired pizzerias

Authentic Neapolitan-style wood-fired pizzerias are increasingly common around Taipei.

02

Try a local topping

Give sweet-potato mousse, corn, or mentaiko toppings a try at least once.

03

Delivery apps

Uber Eats and Foodpanda make it easy to get pizza delivered to your accommodation.

How to Enjoy It

  • ✓Wood-fired spots may have a wait — a dinner reservation helps
  • ✓Check reviews before committing to an unusual topping
  • ✓Confirm the minimum order amount when using a delivery app
  • ✓Solo? Look for a shop that sells pizza by the slice
